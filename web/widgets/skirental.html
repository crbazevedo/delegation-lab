When to release a recovered agent's review budget

A RAG support agent just caught up on a topic — fresh docs landed in its vector DB, so its answers are grounded again and the extra verification pass is now idle overhead. Keep paying to verify that topic, or switch it off and risk re-paying the setup cost when the next organic query shift outruns the knowledge base? Coverage drifts continuously and unscheduled — you can't know how long it stays good — so this is rent-or-buy. Drag the one slider and watch.

Advanced: why exactly 2λ?

This is the worst case — an adversary picks the relapse timing to defeat you. Release too early and you re-pay the 2λ acquire cost on every blip (churn); hold forever and you pay rent without bound. Renting until cumulative rent equals the acquire cost — at 2λ steps — is the break-even no adversary can beat.
